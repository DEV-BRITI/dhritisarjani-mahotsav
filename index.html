About the Mahotsav

Joy, peace and purpose — together.

Sunday, 22nd November 2026. Bring your family to a day designed to delight, unite and inspire.

With the divine blessings of Param Pujyapada Sri Sri Acharyadev and the divine inspiration of Param Pujyapad Sri Sri Abin Dada, the Dhriti Sarjjani Mahotsav returns for its landmark 10th anniversary — a celebration designed to delight, unite and inspire.

This milestone edition marks the auspicious 139th birth anniversary of Param Premamoy Sri Sri Thakur Anukul Chandra. Join us with your family on Sunday, 22nd November 2026, for a lively day filled with devotion, cultural programs and community festivities.

The celebration features powerful performances, heartfelt social moments, and a special attraction from the world's No. 1 spiritual band — bringing an unforgettable blend of music and spirituality to the stage. Come be a part of Dhriti Sarjjani Mahotsav 2026 and step into an atmosphere of joy, peace and purpose.

Words to live by

Guidance from the spiritual lineage.

Three voices, one living ideal — presented with a simple language switch so every visitor can read the messages in English, বাংলা or हिन्दी.

Param Premamoy

Sree Sree Thakur

“Do never die, nor cause death;but resist death to death.”

Short quotation from Sree Sree Thakur’s published messages.

Spiritual Head of Satsang

Sree Sree Acharyadev

“Sree Sree Thakur is our Ideal,the very centre of existence of all of us.”

Adapted from Acharyadev’s published Ashirwani.

Young voice of service

Sree Sree Abin Dada

“Let devotion become service,and let service reach those in need.”

A concise paraphrase of a service-focused guidance attributed to Abin Dada; not presented as a verbatim quotation.
